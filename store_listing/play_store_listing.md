## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Shuddha Sangeetham is the concert-hall companion for Carnatic music lovers.

Find any krithi in under 5 seconds — even without internet. Whether you're sitting in a sabha in Chennai or listening to a kutcheri anywhere in the world, Shuddha Sangeetham gives you instant access to over 20,000 Carnatic compositions, fully offline from the moment you install it.

**Built for the rasika in the concert hall**

You just heard a phrase and want to know what krithi it is. You recognise the raga but can't place the composer. You want to follow along with the lyrics. Shuddha Sangeetham is designed for exactly these moments — fast, quiet, one-handed, no login required.

**Features**

• Lightning-fast search — name, raga, or composer in under 200ms
• Lyrics search — type a phrase you heard and find the krithi
• Full lyrics — pallavi, anupallavi, and charanam for every composition
• Filter by raga, composer, tala, language, or composition type
• Bookmarks — save your favourite krithis for instant access, works offline
• Dark mode — easy on the eyes during an evening concert
• Adjustable text size — from compact to extra large for comfortable reading
• Tolerant spelling — searches for "Bhairawi" find Bhairavi; "Thyagaraja" finds Tyagaraja
• Links to karnatik.com for extended information

**The database**

Over 20,000 compositions by the Trinity (Thyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar, Syama Sastri) and hundreds of other composers. Krithis in Telugu, Sanskrit, Tamil, Kannada, and more. The complete database ships with the app — no subscription, no internet, no waiting.

**Privacy**

Shuddha Sangeetham collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads. Your bookmarks are stored only on your device.

**Data attribution**

Composition data sourced from karnatik.com, the most comprehensive online repository of Carnatic compositions, with gratitude to its maintainers.
